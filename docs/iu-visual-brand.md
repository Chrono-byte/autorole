# Indiana University visual brand reference

Researched October 31, 2026, for Overwatch Esports at IU Indianapolis. This is a working reference, not permission to use IU marks. Check the linked guidance for current requirements, especially the distinction between student organization types.

## Brand colors

IU's [official color guidance](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/color.html) says cream and crimson are the primary identity colors. In most executions, cream is represented as white; black is mainly used for text on white. Primary colors should dominate, with secondary colors supporting them. IU suggests pairing crimson with one secondary color for most projects.

| Primary   | Crimson       | `#990000`   | 0, 100, 65, 34  | 201            |

Hex values are for digital RGB work; use the stated CMYK values for most print work and Pantone for spot-color printing. Check contrast and convey meaning in text or other visual cues, not color alone. [Source: IU color guidance](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/color.html).

### Rivet digital UI colors

IU's [Rivet design system color utilities](https://rivet.iu.edu/utilities/color/) separately list UI tokens, including crimson `#990000`, white `#FFFFFF`, and a different digital black, `#243142`. Rivet's digital black is not the marketing-palette IU Black above; use the value appropriate to the system being used. Rivet also has extended functional colors and a contrast checker.

For a Discord embed's color integer, IU Crimson `#990000` is **10027008**. Embed color is an accent, so it cannot guarantee the contrast of the surrounding Discord interface.

## Typography and visuals

IU identifies **Benton Sans** as its primary typeface, **Georgia Pro** as a secondary supporting face, and **Azeret Mono** for eyebrow text, captions, notes, and other tertiary text. IU's visual guidance favors real people, emotion, context, and the purpose or impact of their work. [Source: IU visual identity](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/index.html). Check font licensing and availability before distributing or embedding files.

## Logos, marks, and student organizations

The IU trident is IU's institutional logo. IU says not to embellish it or change its proportions, and to use approved artwork rather than recreating university marks. Campus lockups are available, but their existence does not itself grant an organization permission to use them. [Sources: IU logos and marks](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/logo-marks/index.html), [marketing lockups](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/logo-marks/marketing-lockups.html).

The organization classification matters: IU says **Self-Governed Student Organizations (SGSOs)** may create their own logos but may not use official university branding; the designated student organization mark can show affiliation and is used alongside the organization's own logo. **University Student Organizations (USOs)** may use the marketing lockup tool and other university brand marks. Confirm this club's classification and applicable permissions before using university marks; do not assume either status. [Source: student organization marks](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/logo-marks/marketing-lockups.html).

IU Indianapolis belongs to the broader IU identity, but campus athletics and spirit assets have additional restrictions. IU says the Indianapolis Jaguar pattern is limited to IU Indianapolis athletic or campus spirit events and messaging, not general audiences; athletics marks require prior approval. Do not treat Bloomington-specific candy stripes or the Indiana state graphic as generic Indianapolis branding. [Sources: IU templates](https://brand.iu.edu/tools/index.html), [athletics and spirit marks](https://brand.iu.edu/iu-brand/visual-identity/logo-marks/athletics-spirit-marks.html).

## Practical starting point

- For a digital club graphic, start with crimson `#990000` and white, using IU Black `#072332` where suitable for readable text; add a secondary color sparingly.
- Use the full name **Overwatch Esports at IU Indianapolis** to identify the club clearly. A color-inspired design does not imply that official marks are licensed for use.
- For role or team colors, retain text labels such as Crimson, White, and Black so meaning does not depend on color alone.
- For a website built with IU's Rivet system, consult its [color utilities and contrast checker](https://rivet.iu.edu/utilities/color/) and [design tokens](https://rivet.iu.edu/design-tokens/).
