## Rules

**1.)** Be kind. Do not harass members of the server.

**2.)** Racism, sexism, homophobia, etc., or any other form of hateful speech will not be tolerated under any circumstance.

**3.)** No advertising (links, etc)

**4.)** No promotion or proliferation of malware, spyware, adware, or any software that can be used to cheat in games.

**5.)** Do not post illegal, NSFW, or questionable content of any form.

**6.)** Adhere to Discord's Community Guidelines. They can be found here: https://discord.com/guidelines.

**7.)** Adhere to Discord's Terms of Service (TOS). That can be found here: https://discord.com/terms.

**8.)** IU's Conduct Policies - https://studentcode.iu.edu/rights/index.html

**Violation of these rules** can lead to the issuance of a formal warning. These warnings will be logged and repeated violations/warnings will lead to disciplinary actions.

## Overwatch PUGs

PUGs are **pick-up games**: Overwatch custom games organized by Overwatch Esports at IU Indianapolis where players group up, split into teams, and play together. You don't need a fixed team to join.

Grab the **PUGs** role in <id:customize>. Watch <#1566113222938071230> for announcements, then meet up in the PUG <#1559714962056937472>. Follow the host's directions for teams and the custom-game lobby; the PUG **blue** and **red** voice channels are there for team comms. Be welcoming and keep it fun for everyone.
